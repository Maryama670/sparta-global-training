# AML Investigation Procedure

This procedure describes the minimum steps an analyst must complete when investigating an AML alert.

## Investigation steps

1. Confirm the alert details and rule that triggered the alert.
2. Review the risk score, severity and current status.
3. Identify all transactions linked to the alert.
4. Identify the accounts involved in those transactions.
5. Identify the counterparties associated with the transactions.
6. Review the customer's occupation, expected monthly activity and source of funds.
7. Compare the observed activity against the customer's known profile.
8. Search for other alerts that share an account, counterparty or corridor.
9. Review any relevant internal procedures or typology guidance.
10. Record both supporting and contradictory evidence.
11. Identify information that is missing.
12. Recommend one of the following: escalate, request information or recommend closure.

The analyst must not automatically close an alert based on an automated recommendation.

## Evidence standards

Evidence should be specific. Analysts should reference transaction IDs, alert IDs, account IDs, counterparty IDs and relevant procedure documents.

Generic statements such as "this looks suspicious" are not sufficient on their own.

## Example

An alert contains three transactions from different accounts to one counterparty. The analyst identifies another open alert involving the same counterparty. The investigator should review the alerts together, compare customer profiles and consult the relevant structuring guidance before making a recommendation.
